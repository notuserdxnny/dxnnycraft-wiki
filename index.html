Getting Started
Quick Start Guide
Everything you need to do in your first 10 minutes on DxnnyCraft.

Step 1 — Join the Server

Connect to DxnnyCraft.minehut.gg in Minecraft Java 1.20.6. Bedrock players use port 19132.

Step 2 — Get Your Starter Kit

The moment you join, type this command to get your free starter gear:

/kit starter

This gives you iron armour, tools, food, and torches to get you going.

Step 3 — Explore and Find a Spot

Use the random teleport command to get away from spawn and find somewhere to build:

/rtp
Tip: Travel far from spawn before settling — areas near spawn get explored quickly.

Step 4 — Claim Your Land

Get a Golden Shovel from your inventory or craft one, then right-click two corners of the area you want to protect:

# Right-click corner 1, then right-click corner 2 /claimlist # see all your claims /abandonclaim # remove the claim you're standing in
Warning: Unclaimed land can be griefed! Always claim before building.

Step 5 — Set Your Home

/sethome home # save your current location /home home # teleport back anytime

Step 6 — Claim Daily Rewards

/daily # claim free money and items every day

Step 7 — Start Earning Money

Check the Auction House for good deals, set up a chest shop, or sell items to other players.

/ah # browse the auction house /balance # check your money /baltop # see the richest players

Economy
Auction House
Buy and sell anything with other players through the global auction house.

📦 Listing Items

Hold the item you want to sell in your hand, then:

/ah sell <price> # list item in hand for sale /ah mine # view your listings /ah cancel <id> # cancel a listing (item returned)

🛒 Buying Items

/ah # open auction house GUI /ah search <name> # search by item name

Click any item in the GUI to open a confirmation screen. Left-click to buy, right-click your own listings to cancel.

⚙️ Rules

Listings expire after 48 hours and are returned to you. Max 10 listings per player at once.
Economy
Chest Shops
Set up your own permanent shop using ChestShop signs.

🏗️ Creating a Shop

Place a chest, then put a sign on it with this exact format:

Line 1: (leave blank — auto-fills your name) Line 2: 1 # quantity per transaction Line 3: B 50 : S 30 # buy for $50, sell for $30 Line 4: DIAMOND # item name

🛡️ Admin Shops

Admin shops have infinite stock. Look for the admin shop stalls at spawn market.

Line 1: [Admin] Line 2: 64 Line 3: B 5 : S 3 Line 4: COBBLESTONE
Tip: Rent a market stall at spawn to get more customers for your shop.
Economy
Lottery
Buy tickets for a chance to win the entire pot. Draws every 24 hours.

🎟️ How It Works

Every ticket costs $50. All ticket money goes into the pot. One random winner takes everything. More tickets = higher chance of winning.

🎰 Commands

/lottery # open GUI — buy 1, 5, or 10 tickets /lottery buy <amount> # buy tickets directly /lottery info # check pot size and your tickets
Note: If you win while offline, your winnings are automatically given when you next log in.

Combat
PvP Arena
Fight other players in the FFA arena. Earn $50 per kill and climb the leaderboard.

⚔️ Joining the Arena

/pvp join # enter the PvP arena /pvp leave # leave the arena /warp pvp # teleport to the arena entrance
Keep Inventory: You are given an iron kit on entry. Your real inventory is saved and returned when you leave.

📊 Stats and Leaderboard

/pvp stats # view your own stats /pvp stats <player> # view another player's stats /pvptop # open leaderboard GUI

Combat
Duels
Challenge any player to a 1v1 duel with diamond gear.

🤺 How to Duel

/duel <player> # send a duel request /duel accept # accept a duel request /duel deny # decline a request /duel cancel # cancel your sent request

⚙️ How It Works

Once accepted, a 5 second countdown begins. Both players are teleported to the arena and given diamond gear. Your real inventory is saved and returned after. Winner is announced to the whole server.

Tip: Duel requests expire after 30 seconds if not accepted.
Combat
Bounty System
Put a price on a player's head. Kill them to collect the reward.

☠️ Placing a Bounty

/bounty set <player> <amount> # place a bounty /bounty # view bounty board /bounty info <player> # check a player's bounty /bounty remove <player> # reclaim your bounty contribution
Minimum bounty: $100. Multiple players can stack bounties on the same target.

💰 Collecting

Simply kill a player who has a bounty on them. The full bounty amount is automatically added to your balance and announced to the server.

Features
Land Claims
Protect your builds from griefing using GriefPrevention.

🛡️ Creating a Claim

Get a Golden Shovel (craft or find one), then:

1. Right-click one corner of your area
2. Walk to the opposite corner
3. Right-click again

The claim is created automatically!

📐 Claim Commands

/claimlist # view all your claims and blocks /abandonclaim # remove the claim you're standing in /trust <player> # let a player build in your claim /untrust <player> # remove their access /accesstrust <player> # let them use doors/buttons only
