Getting Started
Quick Start
Everything you need to do in your first 10 minutes on DxnnyCraft.

Step 1 — Join the Server

Connect to DxnnyCraft.minehut.gg in Minecraft Java 1.20.6. You will spawn in the Spawn World.

Step 2 — Get Your Starter Kit

/kit starter # free gear to get you started

Step 3 — Enter the World

Type /play to be randomly teleported into the survival world. You'll land between 500 and 5000 blocks from spawn.

/play # random teleport into the main world
Warning: You start with 10 hearts. PvP is active everywhere — stay alert!

Step 4 — Claim Your Land

Get a Golden Shovel and right-click two corners of the area you want to protect.

/claimlist # see your claims

Step 5 — Set a Home

/sethome home # save location /home home # teleport back

Step 6 — Return to Spawn

/spawn # teleport back to spawn (blocked during combat)

Step 7 — Claim Daily Rewards

/daily # free money and items every day

Lifesteal
Heart System
The core mechanic of DxnnyCraft — steal hearts from players you kill.

❤ How It Works

Every player starts with 10 hearts. When you kill another player you gain 1 heart and they lose 1 heart. Max hearts is capped at 40.

EventResult
You kill a player+1 heart (you), -1 heart (them)
You get killed-1 heart (you), +1 heart (killer)
You reach 0 heartsEliminated — 1 hour temp ban
You return after banReset to 10 hearts
Elimination: Reaching 0 hearts bans you for 1 hour. When you return your hearts reset to 10.

💊 Heart Commands

/hearts # check your hearts /hearts check <player> # check another player's hearts /hearts gift <player> # give 1 of your hearts to someone /hearts top # top 10 players by hearts

❤ Getting More Hearts

Besides killing players, you can gain hearts from:

SourceHearts

Legendary Crate+2 or +3
Gift from another player+1

Combat System
Combat rules and the combat tag system.

⚔ Combat Tag

When you hit a player or get hit, you are tagged in combat for 30 seconds. Your action bar shows a countdown timer.

Combat Logging: If you disconnect while tagged you are instantly killed. Your killer gets the heart. Don't log out during a fight!

🚫 Restrictions While Tagged

ActionAllowed?
/spawn❌ Blocked
Disconnect❌ You get killed
Fighting back✅ Allowed
Running away✅ Allowed

🛡️ Spawn Protection

PvP is disabled within the spawn area. You cannot attack or be attacked while in spawn.

Economy
Lottery
Buy tickets for a chance to win the entire pot. Draws every 24 hours.

🎟️ How It Works

Every ticket costs $50. All money goes into the pot. One random winner takes everything. More tickets = higher chance.

/lottery # open GUI /lottery buy <amount> # buy tickets
